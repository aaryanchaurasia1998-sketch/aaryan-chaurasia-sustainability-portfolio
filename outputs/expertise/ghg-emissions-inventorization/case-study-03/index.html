CASE STUDY 03 · GHG Emissions Inventorization

Scope 3 GHG Inventorization for a Large Power & Utilities Enterprise

Client Profile

A large Indian power and utilities enterprise operating across multiple segments of the energy value chain.

Challenge

The organisation required greater visibility of GHG emissions arising beyond its direct operational footprint as part of its broader ESG initiatives.

Given the scale and diversity of the business, assessment of relevant value-chain emission sources required information spanning multiple business activities and functions.

My Professional Contribution

  • Supported identification of relevant Scope 3 emission categories.
  • Contributed to coordination of activity-data requirements across relevant functions.
  • Supported review and consolidation of information received for emissions assessment.
  • Contributed to assessment of relevant emission factors.
  • Supported category-level emissions calculations.
  • Contributed to consolidation of the Scope 3 emissions inventory.
  • Supported linkage of GHG considerations with the organisation’s broader ESG priorities.

Outcome

Contributed to strengthening the organisation’s understanding of value-chain emissions and establishing a more structured Scope 3 inventory to support broader ESG, climate and sustainability-reporting priorities.
