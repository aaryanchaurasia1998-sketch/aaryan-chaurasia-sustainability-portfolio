CASE STUDY 02 · GHG Emissions Inventorization

Scope 3 Emissions Inventorization for a Large Digital Connectivity Enterprise

Client Profile

A large Indian digital connectivity and telecommunications enterprise with a geographically dispersed operational footprint.

Challenge

The organisation sought to strengthen its understanding of indirect value-chain emissions beyond emissions associated with its direct operations and purchased energy.

The nature and scale of the business required consideration of multiple indirect emission sources across operational and employee-related activities.

My Professional Contribution

  • Supported assessment of relevant Scope 3 emission categories.
  • Contributed to collection and assessment of activity data.
  • Supported review of information used for Scope 3 calculations.
  • Worked with relevant emission factors across applicable categories.
  • Contributed to category-level GHG calculations.
  • Participated in stakeholder discussions relating to Scope 3 information.
  • Supported consolidation of Scope 3 emissions for sustainability reporting.

Outcome

Contributed to the development of a more comprehensive understanding of the organisation’s value-chain emissions, strengthening visibility of indirect GHG sources and supporting broader sustainability and climate-related disclosures.
