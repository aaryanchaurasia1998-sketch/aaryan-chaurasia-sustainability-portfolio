CASE STUDY 01 · GHG Emissions Inventorization

GHG Inventory for a Specialty Chemicals Manufacturer

Client Profile

A large Indian specialty chemicals manufacturer with operations across multiple manufacturing locations.

Challenge

The organisation required a consolidated understanding of its greenhouse gas emissions across its operational footprint and broader value chain.

The exercise involved multiple emission sources and required consideration of direct emissions, purchased electricity and relevant indirect value-chain emissions.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to consolidation and assessment of GHG activity data.
  • Supported Scope 1 emissions calculations across relevant operational emission sources.
  • Supported Scope 2 emissions accounting.
  • Worked with location-based and market-based electricity-related emissions.
  • Contributed to assessment of relevant Scope 3 emissions.
  • Supported review and application of relevant emission factors.
  • Contributed to consolidation and review of the overall GHG inventory.
  • Supported alignment of emissions information with broader sustainability reporting requirements.

Outcome

Contributed to strengthening the organisation’s consolidated GHG inventory across operational and value-chain emissions, supporting a more comprehensive understanding of its emissions profile and providing inputs for sustainability and climate-related reporting.
