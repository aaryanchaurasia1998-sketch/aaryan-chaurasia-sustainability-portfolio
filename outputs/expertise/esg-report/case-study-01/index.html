CASE STUDY 01 · ESG Reporting

GRI-Aligned ESG Reporting Integrated with GRESB

Client Profile

A large institutional real-estate and industrial infrastructure platform with a multi-asset portfolio.

Challenge

The organisation required ESG reporting that could serve two complementary objectives: communicating its sustainability performance to stakeholders while strengthening the availability and quality of publicly disclosed ESG information relevant to its GRESB assessment.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to development of the ESG Report in accordance with GRI Standards.
  • Supported consolidation of environmental, social and governance information.
  • Contributed to development and review of GRI-aligned disclosures.
  • Supported development of the GRI Content Index.
  • Contributed to alignment of relevant public disclosures with GRESB requirements.
  • Supported identification and closure of reporting-related gaps.
  • Contributed to GRESB assessment activities.
  • Supported strengthening of publicly available ESG evidence relevant to the assessment.

Outcome

  • Contributed to development of a GRI-aligned ESG Report integrated with the organisation’s broader GRESB journey, strengthening the completeness and accessibility of sustainability information relevant to external assessment.
  • Across the broader multi-year ESG programme, the organisation recorded improvement across key GRESB assessment areas, including the Development score increasing from 87 to 96 and the Management component increasing from 21 to 26 across successive assessment cycles.
  • The reporting work formed part of the broader programme supporting this progression and is not presented as the sole driver of the score improvement.
