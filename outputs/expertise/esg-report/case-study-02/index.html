CASE STUDY 02 · ESG Reporting

Integrated Reporting for a Listed Specialty Chemicals Enterprise

Client Profile

A listed Indian specialty chemicals manufacturer with multiple manufacturing facilities and international market exposure.

Challenge

The organisation required an Integrated Report capable of bringing together its financial and non-financial performance, sustainability priorities, strategy and value-creation narrative within a cohesive corporate disclosure.

An important aspect of the reporting exercise was demonstrating how different organisational resources contributed to long-term value creation.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to development and review of the Integrated Report.
  • Supported consolidation of ESG information and performance data.
  • Contributed to sustainability-related narratives.
  • Supported structuring of disclosures around the six capitals.
  • Contributed to GRI-aligned sustainability disclosures.
  • Supported preparation and review of the GRI Content Index.
  • Contributed to integration of material ESG matters into broader corporate reporting.
  • Supported review and closure of management comments.
  • Contributed to refinement of environmental and sustainability performance disclosures.
  • Reporting Structure
  • Financial Capital · Manufactured Capital · Intellectual Capital · Human Capital · Social & Relationship Capital · Natural Capital

Outcome

  • Contributed to development of an Integrated Report connecting the organisation’s strategy, financial and non-financial performance, sustainability priorities and value-creation narrative across six capitals.
  • The reporting exercise also incorporated GRI-aligned disclosures and a dedicated GRI Content Index, strengthening the accessibility of sustainability information within the broader corporate report.
