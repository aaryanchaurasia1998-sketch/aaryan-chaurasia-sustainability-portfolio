CASE STUDY 03 · ESG Reporting

Multi-Year BRSR & Annual ESG Reporting for a Digital Connectivity Enterprise

Client Profile

A large Indian digital connectivity and telecommunications enterprise with a geographically dispersed operational footprint.

Challenge

The organisation required recurring sustainability disclosures that maintained consistency between its ESG strategy, targets, annual performance and regulatory reporting across successive reporting periods.

Its geographically distributed business also required ESG information from multiple corporate functions and operational activities to be consolidated into coherent annual disclosures.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to multiple BRSR reporting cycles.
  • Supported consolidation of environmental, social and governance information.
  • Contributed to development and review of BRSR disclosures.
  • Supported integration of ESG priorities and targets into annual reporting.
  • Contributed to review and presentation of ESG performance information.
  • Supported development of ESG content for broader annual reporting.
  • Contributed to maintaining continuity between strategy, targets and annual disclosures.

Outcome

  • Contributed to recurring BRSR and ESG reporting for an organisation with operations spanning 26 states across India.
  • The multi-year reporting programme supported greater continuity between the organisation’s ESG strategy, commitments, performance and stakeholder-facing disclosures, strengthening consistency across successive reporting cycles.
