CASE STUDY 03 · CSR Advisory

CSR Grant Review Across a Large Multi-Project Portfolio

Client Profile

A large Indian financial institution with a diversified CSR portfolio implemented through NGOs and PIAs across multiple locations.

Challenge

The scale and diversity of the portfolio required structured oversight of grant utilisation, implementation-partner governance, supporting documentation and programme implementation.

The involvement of multiple PIAs with different organisational structures and delivery models also increased the importance of maintaining consistent review expectations.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to CSR grant reviews across a large project portfolio.
  • Supported review of grant utilisation and financial information.
  • Contributed to assessment of PIA governance and compliance.
  • Reviewed utilisation certificates and supporting evidence.
  • Contributed to review of internal controls and documentation.
  • Contributed to identification of governance, financial and documentation gaps.
  • Supported development of project-level observations and recommendations.

Outcome

Contributed to CSR grant reviews covering 50+ projects and 30+ implementation partners / NGOs, providing greater visibility into fund utilisation, governance, compliance, documentation and programme implementation.

The reviews supported more structured oversight of a large CSR portfolio and helped identify project- and partner-specific areas requiring strengthening.
