CASE STUDY 02 · CSR Advisory

SOP & KPI Development for a Large CSR Water Programme

Client Profile

A major Indian corporate foundation managing large-scale water and community-development interventions through multiple implementation partners.

Challenge

A geographically distributed CSR programme required greater consistency in how interventions, KPIs, supporting evidence and programme outcomes were measured and documented across PIAs.

The programme needed to move beyond tracking activities alone and establish clearer visibility from programme outputs to outcomes and longer-term impact.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to development and refinement of programme SOPs.
  • Contributed to development of water- and impact-related KPIs.
  • Supported differentiation between output, outcome and impact indicators.
  • Contributed to development of evidence and documentation requirements.
  • Supported consistent requirements across multiple PIAs.
  • Supported translation of programme requirements into practical guidance for implementation partners.

Outcome

Contributed to development of a more standardised programme-management framework, providing a common basis for KPI measurement, monitoring, documentation and impact tracking across implementation partners.

The SOP and KPI framework also strengthened the foundation for subsequent programme monitoring and impact assessment.
