CASE STUDY 01 · CSR Advisory

CSR Advisory & PIA Onboarding for a Community Water Programme

Client Profile

A large Indian infrastructure enterprise seeking to strengthen community water stewardship around its operational footprint.

Challenge

The organisation required an appropriate implementation partner capable of delivering a community water-replenishment programme.

The selection needed to consider not only the proposed interventions but also the prospective PIAs’ organisational capabilities, relevant experience, technical expertise, governance and ability to execute community water interventions effectively.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to the PIA identification and onboarding process.
  • Supported review of technical proposals.
  • Contributed to assessment of five prospective implementation partners.
  • Assessed relevant organisational experience and track record.
  • Supported review of governance and compliance considerations.
  • Contributed to evaluation of technical expertise.
  • Reviewed community-engagement and implementation capabilities.
  • Supported comparative assessment of proposed interventions.

Outcome

Contributed to the structured technical assessment of five prospective PIAs / NGOs, providing the organisation with a consistent basis for comparing implementation partners across technical capability, organisational strength, relevant experience and execution readiness.

The exercise supported an informed partner-selection and onboarding process for the subsequent community water programme.
