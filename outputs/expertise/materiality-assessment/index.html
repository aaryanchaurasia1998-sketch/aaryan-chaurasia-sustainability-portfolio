A CLEARER VIEW OF WHAT MATTERS

Traditional and
double materiality.

Materiality assessment is most valuable when it goes beyond identifying a list of ESG topics and helps an organisation understand where its most significant impacts, risks and opportunities lie.

My experience covers both traditional materiality assessments and double materiality assessments, supporting organisations in evaluating sustainability matters through two complementary perspectives.

01 / IMPACT LENS

Impact materiality

How an organisation’s activities and value chain affect people, communities and the environment.

02 / FINANCIAL LENS

Financial materiality

How sustainability-related risks and opportunities may affect an organisation’s financial performance, position and long-term value creation.
