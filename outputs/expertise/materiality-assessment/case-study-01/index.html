CASE STUDY 01 · REAL ESTATE & INFRASTRUCTURE

Materiality Assessment for an Emerging Real Estate Platform

Client profile

A privately held Indian residential real-estate investment and development platform.

The challenge

Identify the environmental, social and governance topics most relevant to the organisation’s business model, stakeholders and sustainability ambitions, beyond generic real-estate themes.

My professional contribution
  • Developed an organisation-specific ESG topic universe.
  • Conducted sector and peer benchmarking.
  • Considered relevant sustainability frameworks and industry practices.
  • Supported stakeholder engagement and topic prioritisation.
  • Consolidated materiality assessment findings.
  • Linked material topics with the wider ESG strategy and governance framework.
Engagement Scope
Internal & External Stakeholder Groups
Business Operations & Value Chain
Real Estate Development & Asset Operations
Outcome

A structured set of ESG priorities to inform strategy, governance, policy development, data management and reporting.
