CASE STUDY 02 · SPECIALTY CHEMICALS

Double Materiality Assessment for a Listed Specialty Chemicals Manufacturer

Client profile

A listed Indian specialty chemicals manufacturer with operations and exposure to international markets.

The challenge

Consider impacts on people and the environment alongside sustainability-related risks to the business and opportunities linked to changing ESG expectations.

My professional contribution
  • Supported identification of sustainability impacts, risks and opportunities.
  • Considered company activities and value-chain characteristics.
  • Supported identification and engagement of relevant stakeholders.
  • Contributed to the materiality assessment approach.
  • Supported management and stakeholder interactions.
  • Connected outcomes with broader sustainability, reporting and resource-risk initiatives.
Engagement Scope
Manufacturing Operations
Value Chain (Upstream & Downstream)
Internal & External Stakeholder Groups
Outcome

A prioritised set of material ESG topics supporting sustainability strategy, reporting and resource-risk initiatives.
