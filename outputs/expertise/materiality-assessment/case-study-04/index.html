CASE STUDY 04 · POWER & UTILITIES

Double Materiality Assessment for a Large Power & Utilities Enterprise

Client profile

A large Indian power and utilities enterprise operating across multiple segments of the power value chain.

The challenge

Strengthen sustainability prioritisation through impact and financial materiality, incorporating perspectives across operating functions and internal and external stakeholders.

My professional contribution
  • Supported identification and prioritisation of 21 material ESG topics.
  • Contributed to business and stakeholder mapping across key operating functions.
  • Supported discussions with senior management and functional stakeholders.
  • Developed stakeholder-specific materiality questionnaires.
  • Supported structured scoring of impacts, risks and opportunities.
  • Incorporated internal and external stakeholder perspectives.
  • Supported consolidation and validation of materiality findings.
Engagement Scope
21Material ESG Topics
Multiple Operating Functions
Internal & External Stakeholder Groups
Outcome

A structured basis for ESG prioritisation, strategy development, target setting and reporting.
