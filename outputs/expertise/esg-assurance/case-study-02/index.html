CASE STUDY 02 · ESG Assurance

Reasonable & Limited Assurance for a Listed Specialty Chemicals Manufacturer

Client Profile

A large listed Indian specialty chemicals manufacturer with multiple manufacturing operations.

Challenge

The organisation required independent assurance over selected sustainability disclosures covering a broad range of environmental and social information.

The engagement involved sustainability information generated across multiple manufacturing locations and corporate functions, requiring consistency and traceability across different ESG data streams.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to Reasonable and Limited Assurance activities.
  • Supported review of selected sustainability KPIs and underlying documentation.
  • Contributed to review of GHG emissions and other environmental information.
  • Supported assessment of energy, water, waste and emissions-related disclosures.
  • Contributed to review of selected workforce, health and safety, training and diversity indicators.
  • Participated in corporate and operational-level assurance activities.
  • Supported review and follow-up of assurance observations.
  • Contributed to claim review and engagement-quality documentation.
  • Assurance Coverage
    • Reasonable Assurance: Selected sustainability information, including relevant GHG-related disclosures.
    • Limited Assurance: Selected environmental, social and other non-financial sustainability indicators.

Outcome

Contributed to assurance covering a broad portfolio of sustainability indicators across multiple manufacturing locations and the corporate function, spanning areas such as Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 GHG emissions, energy, water, waste, workforce and occupational health and safety.
