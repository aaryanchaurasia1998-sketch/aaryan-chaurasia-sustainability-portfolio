CASE STUDY 03 · ESG Assurance

Limited Assurance of Water & Waste-related Sustainability KPIs for a Large Consumer Goods Enterprise

Client Profile

A large Indian consumer goods enterprise with an extensive operational footprint and community-development programmes.

Challenge

The organisation required independent Limited Assurance over selected water- and waste-related sustainability KPIs associated with community interventions implemented across different geographies.

The information involved multiple projects and implementation partners, creating a need for consistency and adequate supporting evidence across reported sustainability outcomes.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to Limited Assurance activities under ISAE 3000 (Revised).
  • Supported review of selected water- and waste-related sustainability KPIs.
  • Contributed to assessment of underlying supporting information.
  • Supported review of information received from implementation partners.
  • Contributed to verification of selected reported sustainability information.
  • Supported identification and resolution of information and documentation gaps.
  • Contributed to assurance-related documentation and completion activities.

Outcome

Contributed to the successful completion of Limited Assurance over selected water- and waste-related sustainability KPIs, supporting greater credibility and reliability of information associated with the organisation’s community-focused sustainability initiatives.
