CASE STUDY 01 · ESG Assurance

Limited Assurance for a Large Financial Institution

Client Profile

A large Indian financial institution with diversified banking operations and a significant sustainability and responsible-finance portfolio.

Challenge

The organisation required independent Limited Assurance over selected sustainability information, with particular focus on water-related performance and claims supported by projects implemented across multiple locations.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to Limited Assurance activities under ISAE 3000 (Revised).
  • Supported review and validation of selected water-related sustainability information.
  • Contributed to assessment of underlying data and supporting documentation.
  • Supported review of information received from project implementation partners.
  • Participated in verification and assurance-related activities.
  • Contributed to identification and follow-up of data and documentation gaps.
  • Supported completion of assurance documentation.

Outcome

Contributed to the successful completion of the Limited Assurance engagement and issuance of the associated assurance statement, supporting greater confidence in the selected water-related sustainability information reported by the organisation.
