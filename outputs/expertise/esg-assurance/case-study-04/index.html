CASE STUDY 04 · ESG Assurance

Limited Assurance of Sustainability Reporting for a Global Shipping & Logistics Enterprise

Client Profile

A global shipping and logistics enterprise with operations and business entities across India and international markets.

Challenge

The organisation required Limited Assurance over selected non-financial KPIs reported within its Sustainability Report.

The engagement involved a broad range of ESG indicators and information originating from multiple business entities and geographies, creating complexity around data consistency, consolidation and supporting evidence.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to Limited Assurance activities under ISAE 3000 (Revised).
  • Supported review of selected disclosures prepared with reference to GRI and SASB.
  • Contributed to review of Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 GHG emissions.
  • Supported review of energy, water and waste information.
  • Contributed to assessment of workforce, diversity, training and occupational health and safety information.
  • Contributed to review of data across multiple business entities and geographies.
  • Supported assurance documentation and engagement-completion activities.

Outcome

Contributed to the successful completion of Limited Assurance over a broad portfolio of environmental, social and governance KPIs across a geographically diverse business.
