CASE STUDY 03 · ESG Strategy

ESG Strategy & Roadmap for a Large Digital Connectivity Business

Client Profile

A large Indian digital connectivity and telecommunications enterprise with operations across multiple locations.

Challenge

The organisation sought to translate its identified material sustainability matters into a structured ESG strategy capable of providing greater clarity around its environmental, social and governance priorities.

The strategy needed to provide a stronger connection between material sustainability topics and the organisation’s longer-term ESG ambitions.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to assessment of relevant ESG and sector trends.
  • Supported peer and sector benchmarking.
  • Supported integration of materiality outcomes into ESG strategy development.
  • Contributed to the development and refinement of strategic ESG priorities.
  • Supported identification of relevant focus areas and performance indicators.
  • Participated in stakeholder discussions relating to strategic ESG priorities.
  • Contributed to ESG target-setting activities.
  • Supported development and refinement of the broader ESG roadmap.

Outcome

Contributed to the development of a structured ESG strategy that connected the organisation’s material sustainability priorities with strategic focus areas, performance indicators, targets and longer-term ESG actions.

Illustrative Strategy Cascade

ILLUSTRATIVE MATERIAL TOPICEnergy Use & Emissions in Digital Networks

FOCUS AREA 01Network energy efficiency

Illustrative target

Reduce network energy intensity per unit of data traffic against a defined baseline.

Responsible position · KRA

Network Operations Lead — Improve network energy efficiency.

FOCUS AREA 02Renewable electricity for networks

Illustrative target

Increase renewable electricity coverage across network operations.

Responsible position · KRA

Energy Procurement Lead — Increase renewable power sourcing.

Illustrative example only. Targets and role-level KRAs should be tailored to the organisation’s approved strategy, baselines and accountability structure.
