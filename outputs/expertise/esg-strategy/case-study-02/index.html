CASE STUDY 02 · ESG Strategy

ESG Strategy & Roadmap for an Emerging Real Estate Platform

Client Profile

A privately held Indian residential real-estate investment and development platform.

Challenge

As the organisation strengthened its sustainability programme, it required a more structured ESG direction that could connect its material sustainability priorities with its broader organisational ambitions.

The requirement extended beyond individual sustainability initiatives and included establishing an integrated basis for ESG priorities, targets, governance and future sustainability activities.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to assessment of the organisation’s ESG landscape.
  • Supported sector and peer benchmarking.
  • Supported integration of materiality assessment outcomes with the broader ESG strategy.
  • Contributed to development of the organisation’s ESG purpose and strategic direction.
  • Supported identification and refinement of priority ESG themes.
  • Contributed to development of ESG KPIs and targets.
  • Supported alignment of strategic priorities with governance and policy considerations.
  • Contributed to broader ESG data-management and sustainability-reporting considerations.

Outcome

Supported development of an integrated ESG framework connecting material sustainability priorities, strategic direction, performance indicators, targets, governance and broader sustainability initiatives.

The work provided a foundation for the organisation’s evolving ESG programme and subsequent sustainability activities.
