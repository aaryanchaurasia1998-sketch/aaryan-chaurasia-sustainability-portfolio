CASE STUDY 01 · ESG Strategy

ESG Strategy & Target Setting for a Large Power & Utilities Enterprise

Client Profile

A large Indian power and utilities enterprise with operations across multiple segments of the energy value chain.

Challenge

The organisation was looking to revisit its existing ESG priorities and targets in the context of evolving sustainability expectations and business priorities.

The strategy work was also linked with a broader Double Materiality Assessment, creating a need to translate priority sustainability matters into meaningful strategic priorities and measurable targets.

My Professional Contribution

  • Supported review and refinement of existing ESG priorities and targets.
  • Contributed to sector and peer benchmarking.
  • Supported integration of Double Materiality Assessment outcomes with ESG strategy development.
  • Contributed to identification and refinement of priority ESG focus areas.
  • Supported development of ESG KPIs and targets.
  • Contributed to the assessment and rationalisation of proposed targets.
  • Supported development of strategic actions associated with priority ESG areas.
  • Participated in discussions to review and refine the overall strategic direction.

Outcome

Contributed to the development of an updated ESG strategic framework connecting the organisation’s priority sustainability matters with defined KPIs, targets and longer-term strategic actions.

Illustrative Strategy Cascade

ILLUSTRATIVE MATERIAL TOPICClimate Change & Energy Transition

FOCUS AREA 01Low-carbon generation

Illustrative target

Reduce emissions intensity of power generation against a defined baseline.

Responsible position · KRA

Head of Generation — Improve generation emissions intensity.

FOCUS AREA 02Renewable energy scale-up

Illustrative target

Increase commissioned renewable capacity in line with the approved transition plan.

Responsible position · KRA

Head of Renewable Energy — Deliver planned renewable capacity additions.

Illustrative example only. Targets and role-level KRAs should be tailored to the organisation’s approved strategy, baselines and accountability structure.
