CASE STUDY 02 · ESG Ratings

GRESB Performance Enhancement for a Large Multi-Asset Real Estate Platform

Client Profile

A large multi-asset real-estate platform with a diversified operational portfolio and exposure to institutional sustainability expectations.

Challenge

The organisation sought to strengthen its performance under GRESB by developing greater visibility over existing ESG performance and disclosure gaps and identifying priority areas for subsequent assessment cycles.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to review of GRESB assessment performance.
  • Supported assessment of relevant environmental, social and governance indicators.
  • Contributed to analysis of year-on-year performance movements.
  • Supported identification of performance and disclosure gaps.
  • Contributed to peer and performance comparisons.
  • Supported prioritisation of improvement opportunities.
  • Contributed to activities focused on strengthening ESG performance and disclosures.

Outcome

Contributed to a broader ESG performance-enhancement programme associated with a significant improvement in the organisation’s GRESB performance across successive assessment cycles.

The engagement supported greater visibility of assessment gaps and priority improvement opportunities while strengthening the organisation’s readiness for future assessment cycles.
