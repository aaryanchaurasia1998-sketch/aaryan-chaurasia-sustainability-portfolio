CASE STUDY 05 · ESG Ratings

ESG Assessment Integration for a Large Power & Utilities Enterprise

Client Profile

A large Indian power and utilities enterprise operating across multiple segments of the energy value chain.

Challenge

As part of a broader ESG strategy and target-setting exercise, the organisation needed to consider external sustainability assessment expectations alongside its material ESG priorities and longer-term strategic direction.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to review of relevant external ESG assessment considerations.
  • Supported consideration of S&P Global CSA / DJSI and CDP expectations across relevant sustainability themes.
  • Contributed to sector and peer benchmarking.
  • Supported consideration of external ESG expectations during ESG target-setting activities.
  • Contributed to assessment of sustainability priorities against relevant performance and disclosure expectations.
  • Supported broader alignment between ESG priorities, targets and external assessment considerations.

Outcome

Contributed to stronger alignment between the organisation’s ESG priorities, target-setting activities and external sustainability assessment expectations.

The work supported a more informed basis for strengthening future ESG performance and associated sustainability disclosures.
