CASE STUDY 01 · ESG Ratings

S&P Global CSA / DJSI Support for a Large Retail Real Estate Platform

Client Profile

A large Indian retail real-estate platform with a diversified portfolio of assets across multiple locations.

Challenge

The organisation sought to strengthen its participation in the S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment (CSA/DJSI) by improving the completeness and robustness of sustainability information and supporting evidence relevant to the assessment.

The breadth of the assessment required coordination across multiple sustainability topics and business functions to address information requirements and assessment-related gaps.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to S&P Global CSA / DJSI assessment activities.
  • Supported review of relevant ESG criteria and disclosure requirements.
  • Participated in discussions with relevant business functions for information gathering and validation.
  • Supported identification of gaps across applicable sustainability topics.
  • Contributed to gap-closure activities.
  • Supported review of relevant information and supporting evidence.
  • Participated in discussions across specific assessment dimensions.
  • Supported submission-related and subsequent assessment activities.

Outcome

Contributed to the organisation’s participation in the S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment, supporting improvements in the completeness and quality of sustainability information relevant to the assessment.

The engagement also supported identification and closure of assessment-related gaps and the organisation’s broader efforts to strengthen its positioning against external sustainability assessment expectations.
