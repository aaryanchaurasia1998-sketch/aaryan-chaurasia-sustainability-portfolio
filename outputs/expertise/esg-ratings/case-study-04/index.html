CASE STUDY 04 · ESG Ratings

EcoVadis Assessment Readiness for a Large Engineering & Manufacturing Enterprise

Client Profile

A large Indian engineering and manufacturing enterprise with operations across multiple locations.

Challenge

The organisation sought to strengthen its readiness for the EcoVadis Sustainability Assessment by assessing the alignment of its existing sustainability policies, practices, performance information and supporting documentation with relevant assessment requirements.

The exercise involved sustainability considerations across Environment, Labour & Human Rights, Ethics and Sustainable Procurement.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to EcoVadis pre-assessment and readiness activities.
  • Supported review of sustainability policies, practices and supporting documentation.
  • Contributed to assessment of information across relevant sustainability themes.
  • Supported identification of gaps across policies, actions, performance information and disclosures.
  • Contributed to development of data and documentation requirements.
  • Supported coordination of information requirements across relevant functions and operational locations.
  • Contributed to review of relevant certifications, sustainability initiatives and supporting evidence.
  • Supported prioritisation of improvement opportunities prior to formal assessment.

Outcome

Contributed to a structured EcoVadis assessment-readiness exercise, providing greater visibility into gaps across sustainability policies, actions, performance information and supporting evidence.

The engagement supported prioritisation of improvement opportunities and strengthened the organisation’s preparedness for subsequent EcoVadis assessment activities.
