CASE STUDY 03 · ESG Ratings

CDP Climate Change & Water Security Support for a Listed Specialty Chemicals Manufacturer

Client Profile

A listed Indian specialty chemicals manufacturer with multiple manufacturing operations and exposure to international markets.

Challenge

The organisation sought to strengthen its environmental disclosures through participation in CDP Climate Change and CDP Water Security.

The exercise required consolidation and review of information across climate governance, climate-related risks and opportunities, GHG emissions, energy, water-related dependencies and broader environmental performance.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to preparation of CDP Climate Change and Water Security disclosures.
  • Supported assessment and development of responses across relevant disclosure areas.
  • Contributed to review of climate governance, risks and opportunities and associated information.
  • Supported review of relevant GHG emissions and energy information.
  • Contributed to review of water-related information and environmental performance.
  • Participated in discussions with relevant stakeholders for information gathering and validation.
  • Supported identification of disclosure and information gaps.
  • Contributed to refinement and consolidation of responses.

Outcome

Contributed to completion of the organisation’s CDP Climate Change and Water Security disclosures, supporting stronger communication of climate- and water-related governance, risks, emissions and environmental performance.

The engagement supported the organisation’s broader positioning against internationally recognised environmental disclosure expectations.
