CASE STUDY 01 · ESG Due Diligence

ESG Due Diligence for Large-Scale Data Centre Developments

Client Profile

A major digital-infrastructure business developing large-scale data-centre assets in India.

Challenge

Multiple data-centre developments required assessment of environmental and social considerations across pre-construction and construction-stage activities, while considering both corporate-level ESG arrangements and their implementation at individual project sites.

The assessments also required consideration of internationally recognised environmental and social standards alongside applicable regulatory requirements.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to ESG Due Diligence across multiple data-centre developments.
  • Participated in site-level assessments and management discussions.
  • Supported assessments considering the IFC Performance Standards.
  • Contributed to assessment against relevant Equator Principles requirements.
  • Supported review of environmental and social regulatory considerations.
  • Contributed to review of permits, licences and statutory compliance.
  • Supported assessment of environmental and occupational health and safety practices.
  • Contributed to review of labour and contractor-management arrangements.
  • Supported assessment of land, stakeholder and community-related considerations.
  • Contributed to review of ESG policies and management systems.
  • Supported identification and prioritisation of material ESG observations.
  • Contributed to ESG Due Diligence reporting and associated management actions.

Outcome

Contributed to ESG Due Diligence across multiple large-scale data-centre developments, supporting greater visibility of environmental and social risks relevant to ongoing project development.

The assessments supported the identification and prioritisation of ESG matters requiring management attention as the developments progressed through different stages of the project lifecycle.
