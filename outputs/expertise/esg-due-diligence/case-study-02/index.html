CASE STUDY 02 · ESG Due Diligence

ESG Due Diligence for a Consumer Business

Client Profile

A rapidly growing Indian consumer and food business with manufacturing operations across multiple locations.

Challenge

The assessment required consideration of environmental and social risks across operating facilities and corporate functions, including environmental compliance, workforce practices, employee welfare and selected supply-chain considerations.

My Professional Contribution

  • Participated in the ESG Due Diligence engagement.
  • Contributed to review of environmental and regulatory considerations.
  • Supported assessment of relevant permits and supporting documentation.
  • Contributed to review of employee and contractor-management practices.
  • Supported consideration of employee welfare and workplace arrangements.
  • Contributed to assessment of selected supply-chain and procurement considerations.
  • Participated in discussions with relevant management functions.
  • Supported identification and clarification of ESG matters relevant to the overall assessment.

Outcome

Contributed to ESG Due Diligence across two operating facilities, providing greater visibility into environmental, workforce and supply-chain considerations.

The engagement supported identification of ESG matters requiring consideration as part of the overall business assessment.
