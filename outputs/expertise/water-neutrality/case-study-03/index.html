CASE STUDY 03 · Water Neutrality

SOP Development for a Large Corporate Water Programme

Client Profile

A major Indian corporate foundation supporting large-scale water conservation and agricultural water-management programmes through implementation partners.

Challenge

A geographically distributed water programme required greater consistency in how interventions, KPIs, water benefits and supporting information were monitored and documented across implementation partners.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to development and refinement of water-programme SOPs.
  • Supported standardisation of water-related monitoring requirements.
  • Contributed to development and refinement of relevant KPIs.
  • Supported standardisation of documentation and evidence requirements.
  • Contributed to supply-side and demand-side water considerations.
  • Supported translation of programme requirements into practical guidance.
  • Participated in capacity-building activities associated with implementation of the SOPs.

Outcome

Contributed to a standardised monitoring and documentation framework supporting a water programme involving 16 implementation partners.

The broader programme operated at considerable scale, with reported cumulative water potential of approximately +10 million kL from supply-side interventions and +10 million kL from demand-side interventions.

The SOP development supported a more consistent basis for monitoring, calculation, KPI tracking and supporting documentation across the programme.
