CASE STUDY 01 · Water Neutrality

Multi-Year Water Neutrality Advisory for a Large Pharmaceutical Enterprise

Client Profile

A large Indian pharmaceutical enterprise with manufacturing operations and community water-stewardship programmes across multiple geographies.

Challenge

The organisation required sustained advisory support to assess water replenishment potential, monitor community water interventions and plan for its evolving water-neutrality and water-positivity ambitions.

The programme involved a large number of water-harvesting structures distributed across different geographies, requiring consistent measurement, monitoring and forward-looking assessment.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to a water-stewardship programme spanning 3+ financial years.
  • Supported assessment of water withdrawal and replenishment potential.
  • Contributed to structure-level water-potential calculations.
  • Supported monitoring of water-harvesting and replenishment interventions.
  • Participated in field-level and programme-level reviews.
  • Supported monitoring across multiple implementation partners.
  • Contributed to longer-term water-potential and investment projections.
  • Supported assessment of future replenishment requirements.

Outcome

The programme supported a structured, multi-year water-stewardship journey, enabling consistent monitoring of water withdrawal, replenishment potential and community water interventions.

The client has reported its operations as water neutral for two consecutive years, reflecting sustained progress in balancing operational water requirements
