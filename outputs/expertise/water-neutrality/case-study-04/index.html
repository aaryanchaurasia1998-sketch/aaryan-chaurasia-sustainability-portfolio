CASE STUDY 04 · Water Neutrality

Water Risk Assessment & 2030 Water Neutrality Roadmap for a Multi-Site Enterprise

Client Profile

A large multi-location enterprise seeking to strengthen its understanding of operational water dependency, location-specific water risks and its pathway towards long-term water neutrality.

Challenge

Individual operating locations faced different combinations of water availability, source dependency, consumption patterns and local water stress.

The organisation consequently required a site-specific assessment that could connect water risk, operational demand, conservation opportunities and future replenishment requirements.

My Professional Contribution

  • Contributed to location-specific water-risk assessments.
  • Supported assessment of water withdrawal, consumption and source dependency.
  • Contributed to identification and prioritisation of higher-risk locations.
  • Supported development of future water-demand projections.
  • Contributed to assessment of existing water-conservation interventions.
  • Supported identification of internal water-efficiency opportunities.
  • Contributed to assessment of external replenishment requirements.
  • Supported development of a longer-term water-neutrality roadmap.

Outcome

Contributed to a quantitative water-neutrality roadmap extending to 2030, helping connect site-level water risks with future operational requirements and replenishment needs.

The assessment identified a potential community-based replenishment requirement by 2030, providing a quantitative basis for prioritising future water interventions.
